A warning to parents for their children
---------------------------------------

By Joemarie Sapansa Montales
October 4, 2025
English Translation:

🕊

Saturday, October 4, 2025, exactly five-thirty in the morning, my beloved wife woke me from my sleep so that we could take our usual morning walk — something we have made a daily habit as a couple. We simply walked from our house to Princesa Garden in Jacana.

While walking, I told my wife about the dream I had that very same day, just before she woke me up.

My wife and I were outside our humble home when I noticed something moving above us. It was about the height of a three-story house and was circling around us.

I didn’t realize at first that it was slowly descending right in front of us. I saw clouds as small as a palm, surrounding a white folder and a book with an engraved design or image on its cover.

When it stopped in front of us, I pointed at it and then picked up the book. That’s when I got a closer look at the printed image. It was from the very popular show today — “K-Pop Demon Hunters.” And at the bottom part of it, I noticed a small golden square, about the same size as the gold plate on a SIM card.

Then suddenly, my dream shifted — we were now inside our home, where our two children — a 7-year-old boy and a 3-year-old girl — were sound asleep on our bed. Across from them was our television.

![Image by author (AI-generated image)](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:1400/format:webp/1*lX2MEHJNwGnwlJpcLZBkXw.png)

My wife and I were standing by the doorway of our room. When I looked toward the TV, I saw a man and a woman with anime-like appearances standing right in front of it. The two of them were whispering to each other while pointing at our children.

That’s where my dream ended. I told everything to my wife.

I just want to clarify that I haven’t watched that particular show myself, but my wife has. And according to her interpretation of my dream, the clouds represent the souls being taken by demons (which appear in that show), and the gold color on the book’s cover refers to the title of one of the show’s songs — the song called “Golden.”

This was a warning from God for our family. I am sharing this so that it may also serve as a warning to all parents for their children.
